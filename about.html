OUR POINT OF VIEW

Wonder is where
every mission begins.

Orbit is an independent, educational space-themed website built to make space technology feel a little closer. From robotic explorers on Mars to satellites orbiting Earth, we share stories about the tools and missions that expand what we know.

This project started as a simple HTML website and is growing into a more welcoming, accessible and responsive experience. It is designed for anyone who has ever looked up and wondered what is out there.
